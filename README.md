# Ladles of Love — Warehouse Management System (WMS)

A web-based warehouse management system for Ladles of Love NPC.
Tracks supplier deliveries, purchase orders, drivers and stock intake
for the Nourish Our Children (NOC) programme.

Built by UCT INF3003W Team 22, 2026.

---

## What the system does

- Records supplier deliveries against approved purchase orders
- Captures driver signatures at the point of receipt
- Generates printable delivery note PDFs
- Enforces role-based access (packer, receiver, manager, admin)
- Supports the procurement module (receiving); decanting, packing
  and ECD dispatch modules are scaffolded for future development
